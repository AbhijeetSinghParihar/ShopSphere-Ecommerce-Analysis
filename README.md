# ShopSphere E-commerce Analysis

## Project Overview

ShopSphere is a fictional e-commerce and retail company that sells different products to customers across various regions.

This project focuses on analyzing ShopSphere's sales data to understand sales, profit, customers, products, categories and regional performance. Excel, MySQL and Tableau were used during the project.

## Business Problem

ShopSphere has a large amount of sales data, but it is not easy to identify which products, categories and regions are performing well.

Some areas generate good sales but have low or negative profitability. The analysis was done to find these areas and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Objective

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Understand overall sales and profitability.
- Compare categories and regions.
- Analyze customer and product performance.
- Understand how discounts affect profit.
- Identify loss-making products and areas.
- Provide practical business recommendations.

## Scope

The project covers:

- Sales and profit analysis
- Customer and product performance
- Category and regional performance
- Discount analysis
- Shipping performance
- Business insights and recommendations

## Data Preparation

The dataset contains information about orders, customers, products, sales, quantity, discounts, profit, regions and shipping.

Excel was used for data cleaning and initial data checking. The data was then imported into MySQL for further data preparation and analysis.

## SQL Analysis

MySQL was used to analyze the data and answer the main business questions. The analysis covered sales, profit, customers, products, categories, regions, discounts and shipping performance.

A total of 25 SQL queries were created for the analysis.

## Tableau Dashboards

Three dashboards were created:

1. Executive Overview
2. Customer & Product Performance
3. Regional & Operational Performance

## Key Findings

- Furniture has the weakest category profit margin at 2.49%.
- The Central region has the lowest profit margin at 7.92%.
- Standard Class has the longest average shipping time at about 5 days.
- Some products generate significant losses.
- Tables, Bookcases and Supplies have negative profit margins.
- Higher discount levels are associated with lower profitability.

## Recommendations

- Review pricing and discounts for low-margin categories.
- Investigate the reasons behind weak profitability in the Central region.
- Review consistently loss-making products and sub-categories.
- Improve control over high discount levels.
- Look for ways to improve Standard Class delivery efficiency.
